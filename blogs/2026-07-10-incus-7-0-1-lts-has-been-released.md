# Introduction The Incus team is pleased to announce the release of Incus 7.0.1! This is the first bugfix release for Incus 7.0 which is supported until June 2031. Of note, with the 7.0.x series, we are decoupling the bugfix updates of our various projects, so there won't be an update of LXC and LXCFS at the same time as this one.
